What I see: two separate confirmed instances of the same undocumented color.

1. Hero tagline highlight. In every one of the 12 provided rotation captures (`artifacts/states/home-rot-00.png` through `home-rot-11.png`), the line "physical ai data for the outdoor economy" sits inside a solid filled rectangle behind the mono text. Sampling that fill (e.g. `home-rot-01.png` at x=700-750,y=850) returns `rgb(66,157,109)`, consistently, across every one of the 12 frames regardless of which b-roll frame plays behind it.

2. Helix focus ring. In `artifacts/states/home-focus.png`, the panel carrying keyboard focus (the fishing/beach photo) has a crisp 1px border. Column-scanning x=698 from y=353 to y=364 returns a stable, solid `rgb(65,156,109)` for the entire run, a clean rendered line, not anti-aliasing noise.

Both values land on the same color, and it isn't one of the four locked tokens: bone `#F2EEE5`, ink `#0C0B09`, forest `#16281D` (22,40,29), forest-line `#2C4436` (44,68,54). It's roughly `#3E9B6B`, far more saturated and far brighter than forest-line, the only permitted green.

Why it reads wrong: this is named directly in my brief as the generated-design tell: near-black with one bright, saturated green accent. Here that accent isn't even confined to one moment, it's doing the job of a text-selection highlight in the hero AND the job of a focus ring on the Helix, meaning it reads as the page's real accent color while forest-line sits unused in that role. LOOK.md's focus spec is explicit for this exact surface: "2px solid bone with 2px offset on ink and forest." What ships is bone's opposite, a saturated green pulled from outside the four-token set.

The specific change: replace this color everywhere it appears (hero highlight fill, focus-visible outline on ink/forest surfaces) with the spec'd values: bone `#F2EEE5` for focus rings on ink/forest, and either remove the hero highlight fill entirely or restrict any such fill to forest-line `#2C4436` at full opacity, never the brighter value.
