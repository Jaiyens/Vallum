The path I tried: read IA.md's target sitemap, which puts /dataset and /founder in at P1 as real routes shipping soon, against the site's actual shared chrome in app/layout.tsx, and confirmed against the rendered HTML that zero `<a>` tags exist anywhere on the current page.

What happened: layout.tsx renders no header, no wordmark link, no nav of any kind; "no nav links" is a real, deliberate, currently-safe decision because the site is genuinely one route with nothing to link to. Nothing in the shared layout anticipates a second or third page, not even a linked wordmark.

Where the reader stops: IA.md's own scenario is a buyer forwarding the /dataset URL straight to legal, meaning the first page many readers of that route will ever see is /dataset itself, not /. The day /dataset or /founder ships, that reader has no way back to / and no way across to the other new page except editing the address bar; the no-nav call that is safe for one page becomes a dead end for three.

The fix: add a minimal cross-page affordance (at least a wordmark link home) to the shared layout before /dataset or /founder ship.
