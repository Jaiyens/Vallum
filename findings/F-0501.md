claim: The section's fatality stats never render unless each panel is clicked
---

The path I tried: scrolled from the hero straight through the gallery ("the work that kills.") the way a scanning reader does, without clicking anything, at 1440, 768, and 375 widths, then checked the code paths for every render mode (components/gallery/GalleryPanel.tsx, MobileGallery.tsx, ReducedGallery.tsx, gallery-static.tsx).

What happened: the heading reads "the work that kills." and eight photos appear, each captioned with only an industry word (farming, construction, fishing, west africa fishery, heat, air, logging, the pattern). The actual number for each, "One in three of the world's fatal workplace injuries happens to a farm worker," "At least 108,000 construction workers are killed on site every year," and so on, only exists inside panel.stat, which mounts in StatTakeover.tsx after a click opens it. This is true in all three render paths: the desktop 3D helix, the reduced-motion static grid, and the sub-1024 horizontal row all keep the stat out of visible markup. The only place the number ships without a click is the button's aria-label, invisible to sighted users, and a withNoJsCopy prop in gallery-static.tsx that is never turned on while JS is running.

Where the reader stops: a reader who scrolls past this section the way most readers scroll past everything sees eight photos and eight one-word labels and never encounters a single fatality figure. Nothing later on the page repeats them; Machines are learning this work by watching moves straight to indoor/outdoor footage volumes, not worker deaths. For the two-minute buyer this section exists to hook, the hook only fires for the minority who click all eight panels.

The fix: render one stat per panel as visible caption text so the section's argument lands for readers who never click.
