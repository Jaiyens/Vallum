What I see: in `artifacts/1440/home.png`, sampling a vertical strip down the page at x=0,200,400...1400, the surface holds flat ink `rgb(12,11,9)` for every row from y=900 to y=4773, then at y=4774 every sampled x across the full 1440px width is already flat bone `rgb(237,232,221)`. The change happens between two adjacent pixel rows. There is no diagonal edge, no band of partial ink/bone split within a section, no forest-colored text, no line of copy at the boundary. It is an ordinary CSS background-color change between two stacked sections, at the same sharpness a template's alternating section stripes would use. `artifacts/luminance.json` corroborates this at coarser grain: bands 10 to 41 of the 100-band scan all read 9 to 12 (ink), band 42 to 53 show the Helix grid's photo noise, and band 54 already reads 180, climbing straight to 220 to 234 for the rest of the page. No band shows a partial split.

Why it reads wrong: LOOK.md names this the site's signature: "a bone field rises from the section's bottom edge with a hard 90 degree edge, boundary position mapped linearly to scroll progress... in-flow boundary rests at 42% of section height" and "the turn line's own glyphs flip at the boundary, bone above it, forest below it." The decision log is explicit that the static full-page render is supposed to sample this correctly: "placed the daylight cut boundary at 42% of beat 3's height in static render so the full-page luminance crawl samples the final state correctly without a pinned section." None of that is present. There's also no turn-line copy anywhere in the DOM between the Helix content and "Machines are learning this work by watching" (`artifacts/1440/home.dom.txt` lines 30 to 33) so beat 3 has no distinct content block at all, just an instant surface swap. The one thing this page promised to do differently from every competitor ("Nobody else's page visibly turns the lights on") does not happen.

The specific change: build an actual in-flow section between the Helix and "Machines are learning" with height at least 120vh at 1440 (1080px), place a display-1 turn line inside it, and drive its own copy's color split (bone above / forest below) at 42% of that section's height so the static render shows a two-tone line, not a same-row two-tone page.
