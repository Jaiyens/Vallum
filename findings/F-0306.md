The structural mismatch: IA.md's own done-criteria for the dataset page ends with "linked from the home dataset section" (IA.md line 36, GAP-01). The shipped beat 5 ("What ships with every clip," home dom.txt lines 187-208 in the ARIA tree) contains headings, two lists, and an offer paragraph, and zero link elements. The only two `/dataset` links on the entire page are the persistent header nav item and the footer nav item, both rendered far from this section, one above the fold at scroll position zero and one only after the founder passage and wordmark that follow beat 5 by several more sections.

What it costs: the moment a reader's interest in the schema and consent detail is highest, at the end of beat 5, the page offers no forward affordance to the one route purpose-built to carry that detail further. The reader has to scroll back up past the Helix and hero, or scroll down past the ethos and founder content, to find the equivalent link. For the two-minute reader the brief describes, that is exactly the point in the scroll where a link is cheapest to place and most likely to be taken, and it is the one place on the page it is missing.

What would fix it: add one link at the close of beat 5, pointing to `/dataset`, in the register the surrounding copy already uses (no button chrome, matching the site's rule-not-card language).
