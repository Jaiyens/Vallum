claim: The stat-panel dialog marks main inert but leaves the header nav out of it

The path I tried: read the panel-open flow in components/gallery/StatTakeover.tsx end to end (scroll lock, inert, focus save/restore, Escape, Tab trap, click-outside) against app/layout.tsx and app/page.tsx to see exactly what "behind the dialog" contains.

What happened: on open, StatTakeover does `document.getElementById("content")?.setAttribute("inert", "")`, where `#content` is the `<main>` in app/page.tsx. The site's primary nav ("Vallum Labs", "Dataset") lives in the `<header>` in app/layout.tsx, a sibling of `<MotionProvider>{children}</MotionProvider>`, outside `<main id="content">` and therefore outside the inert boundary. The dialog itself is correctly marked `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`, which asserts to assistive tech that everything else in the document is unavailable while it is open, but the header nav is never actually made inert or aria-hidden. In practice a keyboard user cannot reach it because the component's own keydown handler intercepts every Tab press and refuses focus anywhere but the close button, and the scroll lock keeps the header scrolled out of the viewport, so this is not exploitable by an ordinary Tab walk or a mouse click. It is exploitable by any assistive-tech browse mode that does not depend on Tab or scroll position (e.g. a screen reader's virtual cursor / rotor, which can still land on and activate the header links while the takeover claims modal).

Where the reader stops: a screen reader user exploring by heading or link rather than by Tab can navigate to and activate "Vallum Labs" or "Dataset" in the header while the takeover panel is nominally open and modal, contradicting the aria-modal contract and leaving the panel's cleanup (scroll unlock, inert removal, focus restore) to fire via unmount rather than the panel's own close path.

The fix: extend the inert boundary to cover the header too (e.g. mark the whole `<body>`'s other children inert, or add `inert`/`aria-hidden` to the header while `data-takeover` is mounted), so the modal's background is actually inert, not just Tab-unreachable.
