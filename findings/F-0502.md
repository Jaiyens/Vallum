The path I tried: traced what happens when someone drops a section link in Slack, e.g. a teammate pastes .../#data (or #how-it-works, #rig) to point a colleague straight at the offer, the method, or the rig, the exact "forward one URL" use case IA.md assumes for /dataset.

What happened: components/hero/HeroExperience.tsx:429-434 pins the hero with GSAP ScrollTrigger for `end: "+=300%"`, roughly three viewport-heights of extra scroll distance. That pin spacer does not exist in the server-rendered HTML; it is created client-side once ScrollTrigger mounts. components/scroll-refresh.tsx exists specifically because these positions move after mount (it force-calls ScrollTrigger.refresh() on fonts.ready and on window load). A browser's native jump-to-fragment for #data, #how-it-works, or #rig runs against the shorter pre-hydration layout; by the time hydration finishes and the pin spacer is injected, every id after the hero sits further down the page than where the browser already scrolled, and nothing re-runs the jump afterward.

Where the reader stops: the reader following that link lands inside the still-dark hero or gallery zone, not at the Dataset, Method, or Rig section the link named, with no cue that they undershot and no obvious reason to keep scrolling to find what they came for.

The fix: after hydration and any ScrollTrigger.refresh(), re-apply the initial `location.hash` scroll so anchored links resolve against the final, pin-inflated layout.
