The path I tried: requested a URL that does not exist on the site (/nonexistent-page-xyz) against the running dev server and read the returned document.

What happened: Next's default not-found page renders: "404" and "This page could not be found." in generic system-ui type on plain black or white, nothing else. There is no link to /, no wordmark, no text pointing anywhere.

Where the reader stops: anyone who follows a stale or mistyped link, an old email, a typo pasted in Slack, a future broken internal link once /dataset and /founder exist, lands somewhere with zero way back into the site except the browser's back button. It neither invites another look nor apologizes; it just states a fact and ends the visit.

The fix: ship a custom not-found route with a link home.
