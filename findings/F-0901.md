The exact string: `VALLUM`, in `components/sections/footer.tsx` line 15, a new file this wave, set at `clamp(64px,11vw,160px)` as the page's largest piece of type, in the beat 7 closing footer.

The rule, stated without qualification: "Vallum" must always be "Vallum Labs" (two words). Never "Vallum" alone. This is not a stylistic preference, it is the entity-naming law in FACTS.md's Entity section, and it names the exact failure mode: `vallum.ai` is a real, unrelated company, and a bare "VALLUM" wordmark on the page's biggest headline is the single most prominent place on the entire site where the two names could be confused.

Every other rendered instance of the name in tonight's wave, the header nav, both footer entity lines, the /dataset entity line, the dataset meta title, says "Vallum Labs" correctly. This one wordmark is the sole holdout, and it is also the loudest.

REMOVE THE CLAIM: do not render a standalone "VALLUM" anywhere. Either set the full "Vallum Labs" at display size, or delete the oversized wordmark treatment and let the entity line carry the name. Do not soften this to a smaller "Labs" suffix bolted on beside a still-standalone mark; the rendered word itself must be the two-word name.
