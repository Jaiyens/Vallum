Crop of `artifacts/1440/home.png` around y≈7980-8290 (the rig exploded-view render under "The rig we are building for the field") shows the product photo seated inside a dark ellipse whose edge is a soft, feathered blur fading into the bone background, a vignette/glow halo. `LOOK.md`'s Banned list is explicit and has no photography exception: "CSS gradients, any kind, anywhere, including legibility scrims over media" and "text-shadow, glow, bloom, drop-shadow filters." Whatever produces it (radial-gradient background, drop-shadow filter, or a feathered mask), the visible result is exactly the glow/bloom pattern named in the spec, and it is the only place on either route where this appears. The caption text overlaid on the image's lower-left ("The kit today is 4 to 6 refurbished iPhones on head straps, and this render shows where the rig is headed.") also sits at low contrast directly on the dark photo, compounding legibility.

Fix as a value: remove the radial glow entirely; place the render on flat bone with a hard edge (0 radius, no shadow), matching the "no gradient, no scrim" rule stated for photography elsewhere in Materials. If a caption is needed under the image, set it in the `small` step at `ink` 100% below the image on bone, not overlaid on the photo.
