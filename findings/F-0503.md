The path I tried: walked the Tab order using the ARIA snapshot in artifacts/1440/home.dom.txt and cross-checked against the rendered HTML for any button, link, or other focusable element outside the gallery.

What happened: the only interactive elements on the entire route are the 8 gallery panel buttons (farming through "the pattern"). Every heading and paragraph that follows, "Machines are learning this work by watching," "Consent first, camera second," "What ships with every clip" with its annotation-schema and consent-and-provenance lists, "The offer," "The rig we are building for the field," and the closing line, is plain text: no button, no link, no landmark.

Where the reader stops: a keyboard user's 9th Tab press leaves the document for the browser chrome. This is a known consequence of no contact action existing (F-0011), but it goes further than a missing CTA: there is not one interactive control anywhere in the back half of the page, including across the pricing offer, so there is also no same-page way to jump back up to the gallery or hero (no back-to-top, no in-page link) once a keyboard user has scrolled past it.

The fix: give the back half of the page at least one real interactive landmark, a back-to-top control at minimum, so keyboard users have somewhere to land besides the browser chrome.
