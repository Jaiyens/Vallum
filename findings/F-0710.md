The exact region. The rotating gallery of dangerous jobs types out a line in three parts over real photos of real people at work: "the work that kills.", "the work no one records.", "the work machines must learn." All three show up if you can see the screen, cycling past photos of a farm crew, a roofer, an ironworker. But the accessible name attached to that same heading, the version read aloud to anyone using a screen reader, stops after "the work no one records." The third line, the one that comes closest to the sentence already pulled from the hero for passing a verdict on the workers, is the one line that never reaches a blind visitor at all.

What it made me feel, plainly. Like the softening happened for the channel someone checks, not for the substance. If "the work machines must learn" is fine to say out loud next to a photo of a specific farm crew or a specific roofer, it should be fine to say to every visitor, sighted or not. If it isn't fine, quietly dropping it from the one channel that gets audited, while every sighted visitor still gets the full line, on a loop, over real people doing real work, doesn't fix the sentence. It just moves it out of the part of the page anyone is likely to check.

Why a normal person reads it that way. Nobody scrolling this page with their eyes open gets a softened version. They get all three lines, the same three that somebody clearly decided needed to shrink to two somewhere. The fact that "somewhere" turned out to be the invisible part of the page, and not the part everyone actually sees, reads as fixing a finding, not fixing the feeling the finding was pointing at.

The change as the shape of the words. Either the third line survives, in which case it belongs in the accessible name too, said the same way to every visitor. Or it doesn't survive, in which case it needs to come out of the part everyone can see, not just out of the label read to the part of the audience least likely to notice it stayed.

RESOLUTION: killed by arbiter iteration 2; the aria line-survival decision rides with the F-0709 morning ruling.
