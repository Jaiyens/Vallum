The gap in the argument. The home page's final content lines are "A paid pilot is open: $15,000 to $40,000 for 20 to 40 curated hours." followed by "Vallum Labs Inc. Cape Town, South Africa." (artifacts/1440/home.dom.txt, lines 113 and 115), and the only interactive elements anywhere on the page are the two nav links, Home and Dataset (lines 117-118; confirmed against the ARIA tree, which lists no other link or button on the whole route). The /dataset page ends the same way: "A paid pilot is open: $15,000 to $40,000 for 20 to 40 curated hours." followed only by a "Home" link (artifacts/1440/dataset.dom.txt, lines 43-45). Across both pages, there is no email, no mailto, no form, and no scheduling link anywhere in the rendered document.

What a skeptical reader concludes. A site that spends its entire structure walking a buyer from the danger of the work to the schema to the offer, and then states the offer a second time as its very last words, is a pitch with no reply mechanism. The repetition reads like it is standing in for a call to action that was supposed to be there. A reader convinced by everything above it has nowhere to act on that conviction; the story argues its way to a close and then hands the reader nothing to click.

What would close it. Wire the stated offer to one real way to respond, even if it is a single mailto link, so the page's own close is not the site's dead end.
