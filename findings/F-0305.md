The structural mismatch: the two-route model exists so a buyer gets a fast narrative on `/` and a single complete document on `/dataset` to forward to counsel (IA.md: "the buyer forwards one URL to legal, and Getty's rights layout shows the whole story reads better as one document"). That only works if `/` teases and `/dataset` completes. It does not. Home's beat 5 ("What ships with every clip") renders all 8 annotation-schema line items character-for-character identical to `/dataset`'s "Annotation schema" list (home dom.txt lines 74-80 vs dataset dom.txt lines 13-19), and 5 of 6 "Consent and provenance" items identical too, differing only in two trivial word additions ("for every location," "Non-negotiable"). The capture-kit sentence ("The capture kit today is 4 to 6 refurbished iPhones...") and the collection-window sentence are verbatim-identical between home's Method beat and `/dataset`'s Capture specs and Collection window sections. The offer paragraph on each page states the same $15,000-$40,000 / 20-40 hours / 50-50 split / non-exclusive-default terms, reworded but not reduced.

What `/dataset` adds beyond home's beat 5: one entity/filing sentence (Delaware C-corp, Stripe Atlas) and one compatibility line (Ego4D/Ego-Exo4D naming, which beat 5 already states in different words in its opening paragraph). IA.md's own current-state note calls beat 5 "a compressed schema, consent stack, and offer... a summary, not the document a buyer forwards to legal" (IA.md line 14). The rendered section is not compressed; it is the same document restated, which means the buyer who reads beat 5 has already read `/dataset` in substance and has little reason to click through to the page purpose-built to survive a legal review.

What it costs: the site pays for two routes but the second one earns almost no new information over the first, undercutting the exact rationale (one forwardable document, distinct from the narrative) that justified building `/dataset` as a separate page rather than folding pricing into home.

What would fix it: cut beat 5 down to a genuine summary (what ships, in a sentence or two, plus the offer) and move the full schema and consent enumerations to live only on `/dataset`, so the two routes actually divide labor instead of both carrying the whole spec.
