## Disagree

**F-0406** (critic-eyes, P2, "mono uppercase eyebrow" on HUMAN CREW / ROBOT TWIN). I disagree with the severity and the framing, not the citation. The finding treats this as a typography violation and even says "the color itself is restrained enough not to compound the problem." From where I sit that gets it backwards. The problem isn't the tracking or the case, it's that the page put a toggle in the hero labeled HUMAN CREW next to ROBOT TWIN, before a single word about consent or pay. That is the replacement idea from F-0701 rendered as a literal switch. Fixing the font to sentence case and 14px, as F-0406 proposes, would satisfy the typography rule and leave the actual discomfort untouched. This one deserves tone-level attention, not a font swap.

**F-0604** (critic-voice, "stated direction, not current operations" is a banned X-not-Y). I disagree with applying the no-negation rule here without protecting what the negation is doing. "Not current operations" is the single clearest place on the page where the company admits the limit of what it's actually doing right now, versus what it hopes to do later. That's the same disclosure critic-investor's F-0202 wants surfaced earlier, not softer. The proposed rewrite, "are the stated direction beyond agriculture," is vaguer about whether construction and offshore rigs are happening now or not. A grammar rule shouldn't be allowed to blur the one sentence doing honest scope-limiting work. If the fix has to lose the "not," it has to keep the same clarity some other way, and F-0604 as written doesn't require that.

**F-0401, F-0403, F-0404** (critic-eyes, design-fidelity: missing daylight-cut transition, wrong heading typeface, mono heading at display size). I'm not saying these are wrong on their own terms, I believe the pixel sampling. I disagree with them sitting at P1, the same tier as whether a farm owner would sign this page. None of these three would register with a person reading the page on a phone deciding whether to trust a stranger with a camera. A missing serif font or a hard color cut instead of a gradient costs the page nothing that a real reader would notice or hold against it. Tonight's fix budget should go to the founder-beat gap and the fatality-stat sourcing before it goes to typeface substitution.
